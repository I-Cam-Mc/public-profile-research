# Prepare for a conversation

```text
Use the attached Public Profile Research corpus to prepare me for a respectful conversation with the subject about:

[CONTEXT]

Give me:
1. Their current publicly evidenced context, with dates.
2. Relevant professional and creative interests they have discussed publicly.
3. Five specific, non-invasive questions grounded in their own public work.
4. Topics where the public record is contradictory, stale or likely inappropriate to assume.
5. Names, projects or terminology I should understand before speaking with them.

Cite evidence IDs. Do not suggest pretending to know private information, using removed content as leverage, diagnosing them or mirroring an inferred private personality.
```
