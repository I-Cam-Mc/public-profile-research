# Assess capability for a specific task

```text
Assess whether the subject has publicly demonstrated the capability to perform this specific task:

[TASK]

Success criteria:
[CRITERIA]

Use only the attached Public Profile Research corpus. Build a table with criterion, supporting evidence, contradicting evidence, recency, confidence and evidence IDs. Distinguish claimed experience from demonstrated work. State transfer assumptions and missing evidence. Finish with one of: demonstrated, plausibly transferable, insufficient public evidence, or publicly contradicted. Do not turn this into a general intelligence, personality, employability or moral judgement.
```
